# Logo Design Pro: Minimalist Geometric & Cybernetic Brand Mark Engineering

Use this skill when designing, vectorizing, or generating logos, brand effigies, and app icons for sovereign technology, autonomous AI systems, and enterprise platforms.

## Core Design Laws for Complex Figure Simplification

### 1. The Silhouette & Squint Law
A mark must communicate its essence when reduced to a 16x16px monochrome favicon.
- **Rule of Dual Division**: Split the figure into two complementary visual hemispheres:
  - **Organic / Sculpted Hemisphere**: Continuous classical facial contour, calm eye, smooth porcelain brow.
  - **Mechanical / Cybernetic Hemisphere**: Negative space cutaway revealing interlocking gears, cogs, and celestial orbital rings.
- Eliminate micro-textures (pores, moss fibers, cracks) and translate them into **macro geometric tokens** (clean cut lines, orbital crescents, planetary rings).

### 2. Golden Ratio (Phi) Grid Construction
- Outer bounding circle: D_0 = 100%
- Head contour arc: D_1 = D_0 / 1.618 ≈ 61.8%
- Eye & orbital planetary radius: D_2 = D_1 / 1.618 ≈ 38.2%
- Gear pitch circles: D_3 = D_2 / 1.618 ≈ 23.6%
- Use concentric circular arcs so every curve shares a harmonious geometric center.

### 4. Vector SVG Construction Standards
- **Monoline & Variable Strokes**: Standardize stroke widths to 2.0px (master) and 1.5px (details) on a 48x48 viewport.
- **Path Continuity**: Keep stroke joins rounded (`stroke-linejoin="round"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`).
- **Precision Viewbox**: `viewBox="0 0 48 48"`.
- **Specular Inset Ring**: Surround the effigy with an outer squircle or dual hairline circle with subtle radial glow:
```html
<div class="w-9 h-9 rounded-xl bg-[#1C1B1B] border border-[rgba(212,196,181,0.3)] flex items-center justify-center shadow-[0_0_16px_rgba(212,196,181,0.2)]">
  <!-- Clean SVG Mark -->
</div>
```

### 5. High-Valuation Enterprise Brand Naming Framework
When naming autonomous intelligence and sovereign architecture:
- **Mythological Gravitas**: Echo ancient enduring pillars (Aethelgard, Chronos, Daedalus, Valerius, Palantir).
- **Mathematical Precision**: Pair with systems terminology (Lattice, Tensor, Prime, Vector, Axis, Sovereign).
- **Syllabic Rhythm**: 2 to 3 syllables, ending in a hard or distinct consonant/vowel (e.g. Ae-thel-gard, Syn-tax, Au-re-li-us).
